# LDTD art direction

How LDTD should look and sound, so every asset reads as one mod. Names are in
[GLOSSARY.md](../GLOSSARY.md). Every asset still needs its row in
[ASSET_PROVENANCE.md](../ASSET_PROVENANCE.md).

## Overall

- **Vanilla-compatible.** 16x textures, a vanilla-like level of shading, and boxy models made of
  axis-aligned cuboids. LDTD should look at home next to vanilla blocks without copying any of them.
- **Readability first.** Tower defense lives or dies on how readable it is. Each enemy archetype has
  a silhouette you can identify at 30 blocks in the dark. Each tower's role shows in its shape.
- **Warm against cold.** The player's side (the Hearth, sprites, towers) is warm: ember orange,
  brass, wood, firelight. The Gloam are cold: ink, violet, cyan glow. That contrast is the mod's
  visual identity.

## Palettes

Hex values are the anchors. Shade within ±2 steps of each.

**Hearth side (warm)**

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Ember core | `#FF9A3C` |
| Ember highlight | `#FFD27A` |
| Soot | `#2B2522` |
| Brass trim | `#C9A04A` |
| Hearth masonry grey (blocks) | `#7A726B` |

**The Gloam (cold)**

| Use | Colour |
|---|---|
| Ink body | `#141019` |
| Smoke edge (semi-transparent) | `#2E2440` |
| Deep violet | `#4B2E6F` |
| Mask bone | `#E9E4D8` |
| Eye glow (emissive) | `#5CF2E6` |

Duskglass: violet-black glass with a cyan inner glint (`#2A1C3D` → `#5CF2E6`).

## Silhouettes

| Entity | Silhouette rule |
|---|---|
| Shade | Player height, thin, a long ragged cloak, a flat oval mask. The baseline |
| Flicker | Shorter, hunched forward, trailing smoke wisps (particles) |
| Smother | 1.5× width, huge arms, a tiny mask in a broad body, carries a snuffer hood |
| Moth-wraith | Wide flat wings, a small body, a mask with two eye-holes |
| Dimcaster | Tall, a hood above the mask, one raised arm holding a dark orb |
| The Eclipse | 3× scale, a ring-shaped mask (a halo of darkness), arms like a cloak |
| Hearth-sprite | 0.4 blocks, round ember body, a soot cap, two little tong arms |

All Gloam masks are blank (no mouths) with **emissive eyes**. One rendering trick does it all:
the eye layer is rendered full-bright.

## Technical pipeline

- **Block and item textures:** 16×16 PNG. Generated by scripts in `tools/` (Python, run with
  `py tools/<script>.py`) or hand-drawn. Commit the generator script, so the art can be reproduced
  and reviewed.
- **Block models:** vanilla JSON models with our own textures. A vanilla *parent* model
  (`block/cube_all` and so on) may be referenced. Copying vanilla model files is not allowed.
- **Entity models:** Java `ModelBase` subclasses (cuboids), with textures on a 64×64 atlas
  (128×64 for the boss). The atlas layout lives in the model class and the generator script, in
  sync, the way LDIB does it.
- **Emissive layers:** a second render pass with lightmap set to full-bright, for eyes, embers and
  Duskglass.
- **Particles:** reuse vanilla particle *types* where they fit (smoke, flame). Our own particle
  textures follow the palettes above.
- **Sounds:** original recordings, procedural synthesis (a `tools/` script), or CC0. Vanilla sound
  *events* may be played at runtime, which doesn't redistribute them. Never copy vanilla `.ogg`
  files.

## AI-generated assets: allowed, disclosed (decided 2026-11-01)

AI-generated textures, models or sounds may ship **only** if:

1. their row in `ASSET_PROVENANCE.md` has **Kind** marked `AI-generated` and names the tool and
   model in **Source**;
2. they were reviewed against the palettes and silhouette rules here, and edited to match;
3. the prompt didn't name another game, franchise or artist ("in the style of …" is not allowed),
   and the output doesn't resemble a known existing asset;
4. `CREDITS.md` has a line saying which asset categories include AI-generated work.

Hand-made or script-generated assets are still preferred for anything iconic: the Hearth, the
Gloam masks and the logo.
