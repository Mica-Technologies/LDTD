# Glossary

<div class="grid" markdown>

Breach
:   A block on a raid's route that the Gloam have to break to get through. `/ldtd route show`
    marks them in purple.

Budget
:   The points a raid spends on Gloam. Grows with every raid survived and every Hearth level. See
    [Raids](../gloam/raids.md#how-big-a-raid-is).

Call a raid
:   Sneak-use the Hearth twice, at night, to start the next raid early, bigger and better paid.

Cold Hearth
:   A Hearth whose Flame went out. It claims no territory until relit with coal.

Command Brand
:   The baton that gives Ember Guards their orders.

Core
:   The block at the heart of a tower, which you use to aim, upgrade and salvage it. A Dusk Spire's
    heart is its *Spire Core*.

Dusk Spire
:   A Gloam outpost in the wilds. Feeds and aims nearby raids. Breaking its core razes it.

Dusk Stone
:   The black, violet-veined stone Dusk Spires are made of.

Duskglass
:   The raid currency: what a Gloam's shadow hardens into. Grows the Hearth and upgrades towers.

Eclipse, the
:   The Gloam's boss. Joins every raid on a Hearth once three spires near it have fallen.

Ember Guard
:   A stone-and-ember warrior kept by a Guard Post.

Flame
:   A Hearth's life, 0 to 100. The Gloam at the Hearth drain it. At 0 the Hearth falls.

Gloam, the
:   LDTD's enemy faction: shadow raiders who hunt light.

Growth stage
:   The structure the sprites build around the Hearth at levels 1, 3 and 5.

Hearth
:   The block that claims a territory and holds the stockpile.

Hearth Square
:   A village plaza with an unclaimed Hearth at its centre.

Hearth-sprite
:   A small flying ember-spirit that builds and repairs for its Hearth.

Lit
:   A Gloam standing in block light 10 or more: slower, and takes more damage.

Raid
:   One night's attack on a Hearth, in three waves.

Route
:   The path a raid plans to the Hearth, shared by its Gloam.

Salvage
:   Breaking a tower's core to get half its cost back.

Siege
:   The Gloam breaking through walls that block their route. Only ever inside a territory.

Stockpile
:   The Hearth's 27-slot store. Everything the sprites build is paid from it.

Territory
:   The land a Hearth claims: a circle around it, 24 to 64 blocks in radius.

Tier
:   A tower's upgrade level, I to III.

Trusted
:   A player the Hearth's owner has let build there, with `/hearth trust`.

Watch Post
:   A village's ready-built Archer Tower.

Wave
:   One of a raid's three groups of Gloam, 40 seconds apart.

</div>

The names, and the checks that keep them original, are kept in the
